Arts & Technical Courses Around Calgary

FAN OF: HUMOUR, VIBRANT PERSONALITIES, CREATIVTY, RISK-TAKING, PASSION

Great creators in my mind show, don't tell. There's so many videos about anti-doomscrolling, or self-help crafts etc. While I appreciate the intent, I much prefer to be inspired by watching cool projects, than be told that doing projects is cool.

NOT A FAN OF: CONSUMERISM-BS (dont try to convince me my problems will be solved by shopping), LACK OF ENTHUSIASM (if you're not into it, why should I be?), DULLNESS (fun to watch is important)

Carly B.

Sewist!

The first youtuber that comes to mind when I think of crafty youtubers I love to watch. Based in australia, Carly B is a talented sewist that specializes in colour, patchwork, and whimsical fashion works of art. She is unapologetically enthusiastic about following the ideas that bring her joy, which usually ends up being some combination of bright colours, patchwork, cartoon references, and horses...and her work is nothing compared to her vibrant personality--and that's saying a lot! Every video, she is excited to 'play', to make beautiful works, and to share that enthusiasm, and the peaks and valleys. She is the embodiment of authentically amazing.

Mujin Makes

Woodshop/Furniture/Home DIY

Mujin is a bit fascinating to me. Initially, he may appear to be a 'run of the mill' workshop creator [that is, here is a voiceover of me doing a project and I explain the project as I go and this is the project]. uh-huh. nada. Mujin certainly explains his projects, while being unafraid to present in a way that is deadpan. It is a surprise to realize, shortly into his videos how much he is motivated by humour. A little commitmnent to his videos and you can quickly realize he is an artistic poly-glot of sorts. Extraordinarily talented with making things in his shop, but also, artistically talented, enterprenurial, musically inclined, and all this with an incredibly blue-collar, get 'er' done attitude which is kind of surprising. When things go wrong, he likes to cut in some intense heavy metal music which somehow adds such a nice zest to his videos that makes him stand out amoung all the 'safe' workshop creators.

Caroline Winkler

Home decor and funny

Geniunely my favourite youtuber ever I think. Her channel is now huge, with over a million subscribers, but I was one of the earlier ones, damnit! Caroline's biggest strength is her humour and down-to-earth attitude. She is not afraid to be vulnerable, chaotic, and share with you all her little [incredible helpful] tips and tricks for living your life more 'effectively' I would say...There are many, many self-help youtubers out there, but none have ever been able to resonate with me more than Caroline in this way, because of how effectively she is able to communicate when, why, how she learns something that makes you feel like you are just sitting on the couch sharing a bottle of wine. All this to say, she's a frickin home-decor youtuber! Caroline is excellent at distilling the formulas for home decor in a way that is so digestable and applicable to your own spaces because she can seamlessly mix 'rule of thumb' with an earned understanding of how to create good composition in general for any space... I don't always love her design choices, but I always understand why she makes them, which has taught me how to confidently make design decisions for my own space...and honestly, design choices for a lot of other visual things!

TashuFali

Home decor and art projects

Tashu really came out of nowhere this year and has made such a splash! One of the highest effort creators I've encountered in terms of editing and ridiculous project ambitions. Her videos can make you feel scooped up into 'tashu's world' because of their pace, composition, and unfiltered thoughts of our omnipitent guide, tashu. Tashu's design priorities, approach to projects, and style in general agrees with me more than I've ever seen anyone elses (on the internet or in person, seriously, ever). When I discovered her channel, I was kind of shocked to see that a few projects I had been considering doing ahd already been embodied on her channel (projects i hadn't seen anywhere on the internet, like the multi-coloured resin mirror cabinets!). Her channel is still quite small but I imagine it won't be for long.

Ikoxun's Corner

Hobbies, media

Ikoxun's Corner focuses on the 'indie' side...websites, books, tv, and hobbies. When was the last time you met someone into archery? Ikoxun is a propoonent of simple living and creating beautiful things. In terms of 'vibe', it's a calm, relaxing experience, perfect for those rainy or overcast sundays with soup and tea. She helps you discover the coolest parts of the internet like...neocities! She was the first creator that showed me the world of indie websites, and look where we are!
