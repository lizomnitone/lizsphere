Canadian Space Industry

  • Most companies are based in Ontario and Quebec, with a handful spaced out elsewhere.
  • A regulation called 'ITARS' prevents Canadians from working at most American Space Organizations, including NASA and SpaceX. Basically any company that has a part in defense or government contracts is not allowed to hire a Canadian unless there are special circumstances (like an industry expert).
  • There are still some american companies that can hire Canadians if the ITARS related information is well segmented within the organization, or the organization does not do any defense or rocketry work.

  • CSA (Sainte Jerome, Quebec): Mainly creates contracts for design/engineering to private companies in Canada (e.g. they are NOT like NASA). Can take over a year to get hired there lol.
  • MDA Space (Brampton, Ontario): the largest Canadian space company. Built Canadarm and Canadarm2, and is developing Canadarm3 under a contract with the CSA. After NASA paused the Lunar Gateway in 2026, the plan is to repurpose Canadarm3 for lunar surface work.
  • Sinclair Interplanetary / Rocket Lab: (Toronto, Ontario): designs and manufactures reaction wheels and star trackers, which are used on a large number of satellites. I worked here for a co-op term as part of the Zenith Fellowship.
  • GHGSat (Montreal, Quebec): satellites that monitor greenhouse gas emissions.
  • Canadensys Aerospace (Bolton, Ontario): hardware and electronics for space missions, about 50 people. It was building Canada's first lunar rover until the CSA ended that mission in March 2026. It continues lunar work, including studies for a lunar utility vehicle. They also do electronics for satellites. I worked here for 3 co-op terms.
  • Mission Control Space Services (Ottawa, Ontario): software for lunar and exploration missions, including AI-enabled navigation.
  • Kepler Communications (Toronto, Ontario): satellite communications. I don't know much about them!
  • SFL Missions (based at U of Toronto): builds satellites and often works with private companies (many listed here) on space missions. Strong link between UTIAS and SFL.
  • UTIAS (University of Toronto Institute for Aerospace Studies): Master's and PhD programs that often feed into SFL.
  • NordSpace (Markham, Ontario): developing orbital launch vehicles and a spaceport.
  • Exodus Orbitals (Toronto, Ontario): rentable satellite services, where customers run their own software on its satellites.
  • Wyvern Space (Edmonton, Alberta): Hyperspectral satellite imaging
